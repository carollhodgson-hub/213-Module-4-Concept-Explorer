🌎 Instructor Note on Sensitive Topics

Some concepts in this module involve sensitive issues related to bodies, gender, culture, inequality, and human rights. The goal is not to stereotype any group, but to understand how social norms, gender power, and institutions can shape people’s lives.

Instructor Note: Gender is not only an individual identity. It is also a social structure shaped by families, schools, peer groups, media, workplaces, governments, and culture. As you study, ask how biology, identity, socialization, power, and inequality interact.

🛡️ Understanding Consent

Consent is a clear, voluntary, informed, and ongoing agreement between participants. Consent can be withdrawn at any time and cannot be given through pressure, coercion, intimidation, threats, manipulation, or incapacitation.

Healthy relationships are built on communication, respect, trust, and mutual decision-making.

Reflection: How do communication, respect, and boundaries contribute to healthy relationships?

🌎 Sociological Perspective

Sociologists study sexual assault not only as an individual experience but also as a social issue connected to culture, gender socialization, power, inequality, laws, institutions, and community responses.

Understanding these broader influences helps researchers identify risk factors, improve prevention efforts, support survivors, and strengthen social institutions.
